Meet Nous.

A small inspector with a curious mind. Find what a page exposes, then take a closer look in Peek.

This is a self-contained interactive demo. Its sample image and article work offline. Close Nous with the × to reach these instructions.

  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet.
  2. In Safari, bookmark any ordinary webpage.
  3. Edit that bookmark. Name it Nous and replace its URL with the copied code, including javascript:.
  4. Visit a webpage, then open the Nous bookmark.

Nous scans the page locally. Opening a preview loads that selected resource. Some websites block embedded previews; the Open button gives you a full tab.
